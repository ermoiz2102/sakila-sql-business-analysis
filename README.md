# 📊 DVD Rental Business Analysis (Sakila Database)

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes a DVD rental business using the Sakila database. SQL queries were used to extract insights related to revenue, customer behavior, and film performance. The analysis follows a Metric Driven Design (MDD) approach to align data insights with business objectives.

---

## 🎯 Objective

* Analyze revenue trends
* Identify top customers
* Evaluate film and category performance
* Detect inactive or low-performing assets
* Provide business recommendations

---

## 🗄️ Database

* Sakila DVD Rental Database
* Includes customers, films, rentals, payments, stores, and categories

---

## 🔍 Key Business Questions

* What is the total revenue generated?
* Who are the top customers?
* Which movies generate the most revenue?
* What are the top-performing categories?
* Are there inactive movies or customers?
* How does revenue change over time?

---

## 📊 Key Insights

### 💰 Revenue

* Total Revenue: **$67,406.56**
* Revenue peaks observed in **July and August**

### 🏪 Store Performance

* Both stores contribute almost equally to revenue

### 🎬 Film Performance

* Top rented movies indicate customer demand
* Certain movies generate significantly higher revenue

### 🎭 Category Analysis

* Top categories contribute major share of revenue

### 👥 Customer Insights

* High-value customers contribute a large portion of revenue
* Some customers show low activity (potential churn)

## 🧠 Business Recommendations

* Introduce loyalty programs for high-value customers
* Optimize inventory by focusing on high-demand films
